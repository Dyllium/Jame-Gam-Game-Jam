The game I made is "Small Town Dwarf, Big City Dreams" it's about a dwarf who is in a small town. He has a dream of going to the big city though to drink some of the finest ale!
I decided because he is a dwarf, he would be a miner. So to get to the big city he would mine through the planet.
I was inspired mainly by like dig dug and other mining games. I did finish the game but I couldnt add as much as I wanted due to the time constraints.
